How we work

A clear process, from conversation to launch

  1. 1. Discuss and agree

    Review your goals, existing site and content. Agree a written scope, quotation, hosting term, payment milestones and delivery schedule.

  2. 2. Build and review

    Organise the information, build the pages and review the work with you. Check the agreed journeys on phone and desktop screens.

  3. 3. Launch and hand over

    Check links and enquiry routes, agree the launch, and hand over the relevant access and guidance. Confirm who handles updates and renewals.

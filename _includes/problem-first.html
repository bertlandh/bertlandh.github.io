Four commitments for every service engagement

Understand the problem. Agree the work. Keep the boundaries clear.

You bring the business challenge. We help clarify what needs to change, whether we can help and what a suitable engagement would involve. Here is what you can expect from LearnTech.

1. Understand before recommending

We discuss a recent example, your current approach and the impact on your business. We clarify who approves the work, the available budget and timing before preparing a detailed proposal.

2. Scope the assessment

We use relevant existing work to show our capabilities. If your situation needs substantial investigation, we agree a paid assessment, its outputs and fee before that work begins.

3. Agree before starting

We put the deliverables, exclusions, revisions, acceptance criteria, responsibilities and payment milestones in writing. Delivery starts after the agreed approval and payment prerequisites are met.

4. Approve changes before extra work

When you request work beyond the agreed scope, we explain the additional fee and any effect on delivery dates. We wait for your approval before doing that extra work.

Start with your business challenge

The first conversation is to understand the situation and agree one useful next step. You do not need to choose a technical solution before contacting us.

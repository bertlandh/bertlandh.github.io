<section class="site-section"><div class="site-container content-prose"><h2>Information you send</h2><p>The enquiry form asks for your name, business or organisation, email, service interest and message. Product choice is required for product enquiries when the interactive form is available. Phone and website are optional. The page containing the form is included to help us understand the context. LearnTech Foundation uses these details to respond, understand your requirements and prepare any agreed follow-up or quotation.</p><h2>How the form is delivered</h2><p>FormSubmit processes submissions and sends them to bertlandhope@gmail.com. Its verification process may use reCAPTCHA. The provider's documentation states that submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days; copies sent to our inbox are managed separately. Read <a href="https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">FormSubmit's privacy information</a> and <a href="https://formsubmit.co/documentation" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">service documentation</a>.</p><p>We keep enquiry correspondence for handling the enquiry and any resulting business relationship. Contract or accounting records may need separate retention. Contact us to ask about the information you have sent or to request that we stop following up. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or confidential documents through this form.</p><h2>Contact choices</h2><p>Submitting an enquiry asks us to contact you about that request. It does not subscribe you to newsletters. WhatsApp, phone and email are available if you prefer a different channel; those services have their own processing arrangements.</p><h2>Analytics</h2><p>Google Analytics is optional and stays off until you choose Allow analytics. If you allow it, Google processes page visits, basic browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions such as contact clicks and form activity. It may use first-party analytics cookies to recognise returning browsers. Our custom enquiry events do not include your message, name, email address or phone number. Page URLs exclude query strings and fragments; referral information is limited to the referring website origin. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this integration. You can decline without losing access to any content or enquiry option.</p><p>Use Analytics preferences in the footer or the button below to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal stops optional tracking, removes this site’s first-party Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Your choice is saved in your browser. Reports cover consenting visitors only, so totals are incomplete and automated traffic classification is not perfect. A contact click or confirmation-page view does not prove an enquiry was received. Read <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google’s privacy policy</a>.</p><button class="site-button site-button--outline" type="button" data-lt-manage-analytics hidden>Manage optional analytics</button><h2>Questions</h2><p>Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.data.enquiry.email }}">{{ site.data.enquiry.email }}</a> about your enquiry or this information. This notice was updated on 10 November 2026.</p></div></section>

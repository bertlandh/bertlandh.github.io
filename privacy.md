<section class="site-section"><div class="site-container content-prose"><h2>Information you send</h2><p>The enquiry form asks for your name, business or organisation, email, service interest and message. Product choice is required for product enquiries when the interactive form is available. Phone and website are optional. The page containing the form is included to help us understand the context. LearnTech Foundation uses these details to respond, understand your requirements and prepare any agreed follow-up or quotation.</p><h2>How the form is delivered</h2><p>FormSubmit processes submissions and sends them to bertlandhope@gmail.com. Its verification process may use reCAPTCHA. The provider's documentation states that submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days; copies sent to our inbox are managed separately. Read <a href="https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">FormSubmit's privacy information</a> and <a href="https://formsubmit.co/documentation" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">service documentation</a>.</p><p>We keep enquiry correspondence for handling the enquiry and any resulting business relationship. Contract or accounting records may need separate retention. Contact us to ask about the information you have sent or to request that we stop following up. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or confidential documents through this form.</p><h2>Contact choices</h2><p>Submitting an enquiry asks us to contact you about that request. It does not subscribe you to newsletters. WhatsApp, phone and email are available if you prefer a different channel; those services have their own processing arrangements.</p><h2 id="business-tools">Free business tools</h2><p>The password generator creates passwords locally using Web Crypto randomness. Passwords stay in page memory and are not uploaded, emailed, recorded in local storage or included in analytics. Changing settings, clearing, generating again or leaving the page clears the previous result. Copying uses your clipboard; your device or clipboard manager may retain it. Optional text downloads are unencrypted and include a LearnTech credit. The QR generator encodes the URL or text in browser memory and creates PNG/SVG files locally. It does not upload content, host redirects or collect scans. Anyone receiving the code can scan its contents. Reset or leaving the page clears the current content and output; downloaded files remain on your device. Consented tool events contain only the fixed password_generator or qr_generator identifier and action, never generated passwords, QR contents or filenames. The QR generator does not send content to a third-party encoding service.</p><p>The SVG converter and favicon generator decode your selected JPEG, PNG or WebP in browser memory. The SVG converter traces a reduced-resolution image in a local worker; the favicon generator creates icons and a ZIP locally. Neither uploads your image, brand name, vector paths or generated files, and no image history is saved. Reset clears the session. Downloads stay on your device. SVG downloads contain a visible LearnTech credit footer; favicon packages include branded filenames, PNG credit metadata, a manifest description and a LearnTech README. Consented analytics sends only the fixed svg_converter or favicon tool identifier and action, never your image, brand name, filenames or output. The tracing library is served from this website, not fetched from a third-party CDN at runtime.</p><p>BrandSnap processes your selected photo, logo and text in browser memory on your device. It does not upload or save them, or keep an image history. Optional camera access starts only when you press Start camera, requests video without audio or location, and stops when you capture, stop, reset, hide the tab or leave the page. Downloads include a visible LearnTech credit strip. Reset everything clears the current session; downloaded files remain on your device. With analytics consent, BrandSnap sends only the fixed brandsnap tool identifier and action, never images, filenames, text or camera data.</p><p>The image compressor and resizer reads a JPEG, PNG or WebP you select and processes it in browser memory on your device. It does not upload your image to LearnTech or an image-processing service. It does not save image history or original filenames in local storage. The finished image includes a visible LearnTech credit strip. Use Remove image or leave the page to release the current image; downloaded files are managed by your browser and device. Our custom analytics events include only the fixed image_tools identifier and action name, never your image, filename or its dimensions.</p><p>The invoice and quotation tool processes your entries in your browser. It does not automatically upload your business details, customer information, line items or payment instructions to LearnTech. Choosing Save on this device stores document data in this browser profile using local storage; this is not encrypted cloud storage. Other people using the same browser profile may access it. Browser cleanup can erase it. You can delete selected saved documents and download or import editable JSON. Password-protected downloads use authenticated encryption in your browser; keep the password separately because LearnTech cannot recover it. Plain JSON and browser saves are not encrypted. Imported files are validated and offered for review before being saved. Printing and downloading do not send the contents to LearnTech.</p><p>The website checker sends the public page address you enter to LearnTech’s hosted checking service on ChatGPT Sites / Cloudflare. The service checks public DNS records, crawl rules, one public page and selected response headers. Do not submit private links, passwords or addresses containing query strings. The checker stores no customer account or lead list; public-page reports may be cached for up to 10 minutes to reduce repeated requests. Its hosting providers may process request metadata and operational logs. It does not verify backups, exploit vulnerabilities or access logged-in pages.</p><p>Email reports are optional. Before submitting, you can preview the exact report. If you choose email delivery, your email address, report contents, tool name and follow-up choice are sent to FormSubmit, which sends a copy to you and LearnTech’s inbox. Invoice reports include the business and customer details you entered; check that you are entitled to share them. FormSubmit may use reCAPTCHA, and its documentation states that submissions are archived for 30 days. LearnTech uses the copy to fulfil and handle your report request, and follows up about assistance only if you select the separate optional checkbox. This is not newsletter consent. Contact us to request deletion of a report copy, subject to any records we need for an agreed business relationship.</p><p>Optional analytics records tool starts, completed reports, save/download/print actions, errors, email-report requests and LearnTech credit-link clicks using only a fixed tool identifier. Your website address, document contents, report, filenames and email address are not sent in these custom analytics events. The tools work when analytics is off. An email-report request event does not prove delivery.</p><p>The email signature builder keeps entered details in page memory and does not send them to LearnTech. In the invoice, BrandSnap and email-signature tools, Reusable business details lets you explicitly save a shared business profile in local storage on this device. This optional profile contains only the contact and brand fields you enter, is not encrypted, and is not automatically applied. Anyone using this browser profile may see it. Remove saved profile deletes that profile without deleting invoices or uploaded images. Passwords, customer records and uploaded photos are not included. Logo or photo URLs load images directly from the chosen HTTPS host. Copied signatures and downloads include your entered details and LearnTech credit. Consent-based tool events do not include signature content or contact information.</p><p>The passport-photo tool processes photo files and camera captures in browser memory. It does not upload, save an online photo history or send photo content to analytics. Camera access starts only when you choose Start camera and allow permission, and stops after capture, on Stop camera, when the camera panel closes or when the page is left. Downloads and saved PDFs remain on your device. Remove photo and reset clears the current working image.</p><h2>Analytics</h2><p>Google Analytics is optional and stays off until you choose Allow analytics. If you allow it, Google processes page visits, basic browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions such as contact clicks, form activity and business-tool actions. It may use first-party analytics cookies to recognise returning browsers. Our custom enquiry events do not include your message, name, email address or phone number. Page URLs exclude query strings and fragments; referral information is limited to the referring website origin. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this integration. You can decline without losing access to any content or enquiry option.</p><p>Use Analytics preferences in the footer or the button below to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal stops optional tracking, removes this site’s first-party Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Your choice is saved in your browser. Reports cover consenting visitors only, so totals are incomplete and automated traffic classification is not perfect. A contact click or confirmation-page view does not prove an enquiry was received.

<section class="site-section"><div class="site-container content-prose"><h2>Information you send</h2><p>The enquiry form asks for your name, business or organisation, email, service interest and message. Product choice is required for product enquiries when the interactive form is available. Phone and website are optional. The page containing the form is included to help us understand the context. LearnTech Foundation uses these details to respond, understand your requirements and prepare any agreed follow-up or quotation.</p><h2>How the form is delivered</h2><p>FormSubmit processes submissions and sends them to bertlandhope@gmail.com. Its verification process may use reCAPTCHA. The provider's documentation states that submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days; copies sent to our inbox are managed separately. Read <a href="https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">FormSubmit's privacy information</a> and <a href="https://formsubmit.co/documentation" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">service documentation</a>.</p><p>We keep enquiry correspondence for handling the enquiry and any resulting business relationship. Contract or accounting records may need separate retention. Contact us to ask about the information you have sent or to request that we stop following up. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or confidential documents through this form.</p><h2>Contact choices</h2><p>Submitting an enquiry asks us to contact you about that request. It does not subscribe you to newsletters. WhatsApp, phone and email are available if you prefer a different channel; those services have their own processing arrangements.</p><h2 id="business-tools">Free business tools</h2><p>The invoice and quotation tool processes your entries in your browser. It does not automatically upload your business details, customer information, line items or payment instructions to LearnTech. Choosing Save on this device stores document data in this browser profile using local storage; this is not encrypted cloud storage. Other people using the same browser profile may access it. Browser cleanup can erase it. You can delete selected saved documents and download or restore a JSON backup. Printing and downloading do not send the contents to LearnTech.</p><p>The website checker sends the public page address you enter to LearnTech’s hosted checking service on ChatGPT Sites / Cloudflare. The service checks public DNS records, crawl rules, one public page and selected response headers. Do not submit private links, passwords or addresses containing query strings. The checker stores no customer account or lead list; public-page reports may be cached for up to 10 minutes to reduce repeated requests. Its hosting providers may process request metadata and operational logs. It does not verify backups, exploit vulnerabilities or access logged-in pages.</p><p>Email reports are optional. Before submitting, you can preview the exact report. If you choose email delivery, your email address, report contents, tool name and follow-up choice are sent to FormSubmit, which sends a copy to you and LearnTech’s inbox. Invoice reports include the business and customer details you entered; check that you are entitled to share them. FormSubmit may use reCAPTCHA, and its documentation states that submissions are archived for 30 days. LearnTech uses the copy to fulfil and handle your report request, and follows up about assistance only if you select the separate optional checkbox. This is not newsletter consent. Contact us to request deletion of a report copy, subject to any records we need for an agreed business relationship.</p><p>Optional analytics records tool starts, completed reports, save/download/print actions, errors and email-report requests using only a fixed tool identifier. Your website address, document contents, report, filenames and email address are not sent in these custom analytics events. The tools work when analytics is off. An email-report request event does not prove delivery.</p><h2>Analytics</h2><p>Google Analytics is optional and stays off until you choose Allow analytics. If you allow it, Google processes page visits, basic browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions such as contact clicks, form activity and business-tool actions. It may use first-party analytics cookies to recognise returning browsers. Our custom enquiry events do not include your message, name, email address or phone number. Page URLs exclude query strings and fragments; referral information is limited to the referring website origin. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this integration. You can decline without losing access to any content or enquiry option.</p><p>Use Analytics preferences in the footer or the button below to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal stops optional tracking, removes this site’s first-party Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Your choice is saved in your browser. Reports cover consenting visitors only, so totals are incomplete and automated traffic classification is not perfect. A contact click or confirmation-page view does not prove an enquiry was received. Read <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google’s privacy policy</a>.</p><button class="site-button site-button--outline" type="button" data-lt-manage-analytics hidden>Manage optional analytics</button><h2>Questions</h2><p>Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.data.enquiry.email }}">{{ site.data.enquiry.email }}</a> about your enquiry or this information.

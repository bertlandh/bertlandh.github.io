<section class="site-section"><div class="site-container content-prose"><h2>Information you send</h2><p>The enquiry form asks for your name, business or organisation, email, service interest and message. Product choice is required for product enquiries when the interactive form is available. Phone and website are optional. The page containing the form is included to help us understand the context. LearnTech Foundation uses these details to respond, understand your requirements and prepare any agreed follow-up or quotation.</p><h2>How the form is delivered</h2><p>FormSubmit processes submissions and sends them to bertlandhope@gmail.com. Its verification process may use reCAPTCHA. The provider's documentation states that submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days; copies sent to our inbox are managed separately. Read <a href="https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">FormSubmit's privacy information</a> and <a href="https://formsubmit.co/documentation" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">service documentation</a>.</p><p>We keep enquiry correspondence for handling the enquiry and any resulting business relationship. Contract or accounting records may need separate retention. Contact us to ask about the information you have sent or to request that we stop following up. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or confidential documents through this form.</p><h2>Contact choices</h2><p>Submitting an enquiry asks us to contact you about that request. It does not subscribe you to newsletters. WhatsApp, phone and email are available if you prefer a different channel; those services have their own processing arrangements.</p><h2>Analytics</h2><p>Google Analytics is currently switched off. No enquiry message, name, email address or phone number is included in the site's optional analytics events. If analytics is enabled in future, the site will ask for your choice before loading it.</p><button class="site-button site-button--outline" type="button" data-lt-manage-analytics hidden>Manage optional analytics</button><h2>Questions</h2><p>Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.data.enquiry.email }}">{{ site.data.enquiry.email }}</a> about your enquiry or this information. This notice was updated on 10 November 2026.</p></div></section>

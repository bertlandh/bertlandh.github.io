A contact click or submit attempt is not counted as a confirmed customer.</p><h2>Search discovery and release checks</h2><p>Page titles, descriptions, canonical links, crawler instructions and a sitemap support discovery. This is LearnTech’s own website, not a commissioned client engagement.

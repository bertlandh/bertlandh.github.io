Create an email signature for your business, freelance work or creator profile. Details stay in this page; no account or upload required.

Your details

Only your name is required. Leave anything else blank to omit it.

For a logo or photo, use an image hosted on your own HTTPS website. Adding its address loads it from that host in the preview and may load it when recipients view emails. Recipients can block remote images. No local image upload is needed.

Your live preview

Add your name to begin.

The LearnTech credit stays in copied and downloaded signatures. Email apps can change spacing and fonts; send yourself a test message before using it widely.

Add it to your email

  1. Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature. Create a signature, paste, select your defaults and save changes.
  2. Outlook: Open Settings and search for Signatures. Create a signature, paste and choose your defaults. In classic Outlook, use File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  3. Apple Mail on Mac: Mail → Settings → Signatures. Add a signature to your account and paste it. If formatting changes, uncheck “Always match my default message font”.

If formatted copying is unavailable, download the HTML, open it in your browser, select the displayed signature and copy. Mobile email apps may accept only plain text. Test links and images in your own email app.

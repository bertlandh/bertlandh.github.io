Prepare passport-size or ID photos on your device. Upload a photo, take one with your phone, or capture it with a webcam. Photos are never uploaded or included in analytics.

This is a sizing and printing tool, not an official passport-photo validator. Check your destination’s current rules. Read Jamaica’s PICA photo requirements, including background, pose and photo quality.

Photo preview & guides

Oval: centre your head, top to chin.

Blue: head top at 3.5 mm.

Green: chin 25–35 mm below the blue line.

Red: 2 mm minimum above the head.

Alignment help & photo rules

35 mm wide × 45 mm high. Align the top of the head (including hair) with the blue 3.5 mm line. Zoom and move the photo until the chin falls between the two green lines: 25–35 mm below the head-top line.

The oval is an approximate positioning aid, not an official face-width boundary. Keep your full head and both sides of your face visible; use the measurement lines to check head height. These are manual guides, not automatic face measurements. Zoom and position the photo using the controls. Guides never appear in downloads.

Compare with PICA’s requirements

  • Face the camera straight on, with a neutral expression, eyes visible, and head, neck and shoulders in view.
  • Use a plain, evenly lit background without shadows or other people. Check PICA’s background and clothing guidance for your circumstances.
  • Use a recent, sharp colour photo and check the required paper and finish. This tool does not retouch or certify photos.

PICA photo requirements · PICA illustrated photo guide (PDF)

PICA’s web page gives 3–4 mm headroom; its poster gives at least 2 mm. The blue target uses 3.5 mm and the red line shows the poster’s minimum. Confirm current requirements with PICA if unsure. LearnTech is independent of PICA.

1. Choose your photo

Still JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB and 24 megapixels. Export HEIC photos as JPEG first.

One photo picker works on phones and computers. To take a new photo, open the camera option below.

Or take a photo with your phone or computer camera

Camera access starts only when you choose Start camera and allow permission. Capture is not mirrored; check your pose.

2. Set the size and crop

The size presets set dimensions only. They do not check any country’s acceptance requirements.

Choose a photo to see the available crop resolution.

3. Arrange your print sheet

A 4 mm gap and faint cut borders separate the photos. Credit and print instructions sit outside the photos. Print as many sheets as needed.

Choose a photo to begin.

Your printable sheet

Choose the matching paper, portrait orientation, 100% / Actual size, no margins, no headers or footers. Disable Fit to page. Measure a test photo with a ruler before cutting.

Keep the photo at its real size.

Letter paper is 215.9 × 279.4 mm. A 35 × 45 mm photo is approximately 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI. Filling the sheet adds copies without making the photos larger. The PNG includes a 300 DPI density tag; your print app can still override it. Printable HTML uses physical millimetre dimensions.

The tool crops without stretching and warns if the crop has fewer pixels than the 300 DPI target. It does not improve a blurry photo, remove its background, retouch your face or validate biometric requirements. For an official application, use a suitable original photo and the paper/finish required by the issuing authority.

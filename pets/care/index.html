A thoughtful
beginning.

A bird is a living animal, with needs that continue every day. Use these questions to prepare before buying.

Your before-you-buy checklist

  • Do you have suitable, secure housing, ventilation and a safe environment?
  • Can you provide appropriate food, clean water, cleaning and daily attention for this species?
  • Have you considered noise, lifespan, other pets and everyone in your household?
  • Can you budget for ongoing supplies and veterinary care?
  • Have you checked sourcing, any relevant documentation and safe collection?

Ask about the individual bird

Confirm age where known, diet, handling experience, care requirements and any relevant health information. A description is not a veterinary diagnosis. Seek a qualified veterinarian for health concerns.

Bird eggs need specific information

Confirm the species and whether eggs are intended for hatching or another stated purpose. Ask about collection dates, storage, transport and incubation. Never assume fertility or a successful hatch is guaranteed.

Sourcing, permits & movement

Requirements depend on the species, source and proposed movement. A listing does not establish permission to possess, trade, import or ship an animal. Confirm the relevant requirements before agreeing to a purchase.

For imports, consult Jamaica’s Veterinary Services Division. For wildlife and protected species, consult NEPA. No international or islandwide live-animal shipment is offered by this website.

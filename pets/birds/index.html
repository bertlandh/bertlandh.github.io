What would you like to ask about?

Our enquiries include lovebirds, budgies, quail, diamond doves, zebra finches and green-cheek conures. Availability changes; confirm the individual birds, current photos and collection details directly.

Bird pair-price guides

The supplied prices are J$12,000 per pair for diamond doves, J$7,000 per pair for button quail and J$6,000 per pair for zebra finches. These guides do not reserve a pair or confirm current stock.

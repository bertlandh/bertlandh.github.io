Meet PrettiPetz

Built around
a love of animals.

We’re a new bird and pet business in Jamaica, bringing together hands-on animal knowledge and a straightforward online experience.

One partner focuses on birds, breeding, care and sourcing. The other builds the technology that helps you explore what’s available and get in touch.

A clear picture before you choose.

Our catalogue is being prepared around actual photographs, Jamaican-dollar prices and relevant care information. Ask questions, discuss suitability and confirm collection arrangements before making a purchase.

Taking the next step thoughtfully.

We’re starting with birds, bird eggs and pet supplies, and will build on that foundation as the business grows.

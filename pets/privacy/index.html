Your information

Privacy.

This website currently provides catalogue information and links for enquiries. It has no customer account, checkout or website enquiry form.

WhatsApp enquiries

When enabled, an enquiry button opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Nothing is sent until you send it. WhatsApp processes information under its own privacy terms. Share only what is necessary for your enquiry.

Browsing and analytics

No advertising tags or analytics trackers are loaded by this site. The hosting provider may process standard request logs. Search and category filters run in your browser; filter text may appear in the page URL, so do not enter personal information in catalogue searches.

Business records and questions

Before orders begin, we will publish customer contact details and the information needed about handling order records, retention and privacy requests.

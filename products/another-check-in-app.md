Teams may need to understand punctuality, managed locations, supporting location evidence, approved itineraries, leave, exceptions, and monthly reporting.</p><p>Another Check-In App connects these workflows so staff can record activity and authorised reviewers can understand what happened.</p><p>This is a product in development. <article class="site-card"><h3>Attendance and punctuality</h3><p>Record check-in and check-out, review attendance history, and evaluate arrivals and departures against applicable work times.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Location-aware activity</h3><p>Use managed locations, GPS evidence, geofence evaluation, exceptions, and QR or barcode location check-in.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Reports and registers</h3><p>Review attendance and punctuality, create monthly registers, and produce spreadsheet, CSV, and printable output.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Itineraries</h3><p>Submit and approve itineraries, then compare planned activity with actual check-ins and review deviations.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Leave administration</h3><p>Manage policies, allocations, requests, approvals, balances, history, and authorised reporting.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Mobile-oriented PWA</h3><p>Install the app on supported devices and retain supported pending check-ins offline for later synchronisation and server validation.</p></article>

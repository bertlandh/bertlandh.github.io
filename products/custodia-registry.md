Without a connected view, finding a record and reconstructing its history becomes harder than it should be.</p><p>Custodia Registry brings registration, location, physical custody, digitised files, access, and review into one operational workspace.</p></div><aside class="product-value"><strong>One connected record history</strong><span>See structured record details, location, holder, movement, files, requests, and activity in context.</span></aside></div></section>

<section class="site-section site-section--warm"><div class="site-container"><div class="section-heading"><p class="section-heading__eyebrow">Key capabilities</p><h2>Support the record through its working life</h2></div><div class="capability-grid">
<article class="site-card"><h3>Register and find</h3><p>Create structured record information, search and filter the register, and import or export metadata.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Track physical custody</h3><p>Manage requests, checkout, pickup, recall, return receipt, final reshelving, and the resulting custody history.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Connect digital files</h3><p>Store digitised-file metadata, previews, versions, and controlled digital-access workflows alongside the record.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Organise locations</h3><p>Describe storage locations consistently and use QR record and location labels to assist handling and reshelving.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Review and govern</h3><p>Use roles and permissions, audit history, retention and disposition workflows, and human-reviewed duplicate cases.</p></article>
<article class="site-card"><h3>Report and export</h3><p>Review operational activity, physical loans, retention, categories, and departments through reports and exports.</p></article>
